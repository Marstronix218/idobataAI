# Generation and cost summary

## Completed Sora run

- Model: `sora-2-pro`, fallback `sora-2`
- Resolution: 1024x1792
- Duration: 4 seconds per generation
- Shots: 6
- Initial variants: 2 per shot
- Generated duration: 48 seconds
- Completed clips: 12 (two variants for each of six shots)
- Model actually used: `sora-2-pro` for all 12 clips
- Estimated Sora 2 Pro cost at $0.50/generated second: **approximately $24.00**

The generator used the repository's gitignored `AI_API_KEY` as the supported local alias. One transient 503 and one transient 504 occurred while polling; the existing jobs were resumed and no duplicate generations were submitted.

## Built-in fallback imagery

Six project-bound cinematic stills were generated with the built-in image-generation surface. They are retained only as historical timing/design fallbacks and are not represented as Sora output.
